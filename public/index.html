Mind Meld

Choose a word. Find the middle ground. Meld minds.

or

How to play
  1. Submit in secret. Everyone types any word that comes to mind. Words stay hidden until all players are ready.
  2. Reveal the words. All words appear at the same time. If everyone typed the exact same word, you've melded minds!
  3. Find the middle ground. No match? Think of a concept, category or association where everyone's words meet, then submit again. Repeat until you all land on the same word.

Pro tip: Don't try to be clever or pick obscure references. Ask yourself: "What's the most obvious word everyone else will think of?"

Quick example

  • Round 1: You say Campfire, your friend says Sweet. No match.
  • Round 2: What obvious word connects Campfire and Sweet? You both say Marshmallow. Mind melded!
